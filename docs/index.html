The details.

Where are my recordings saved?

In your Mac’s Application Support folder, as audio, transcript, metadata, and generated output files. The files are yours to copy or back up.

Does transcription need an internet connection?

No. Transcription uses Apple’s on-device Speech framework. Generated notes use the provider you select; Apple Intelligence and Ollama can run locally, while OpenRouter uses a network connection.

What Mac do I need?

Muffin requires macOS 26 or later, plus microphone and Speech Recognition permissions. Some generation options depend on the model provider you choose.

Is there a subscription or an account?

Muffin needs no account and has no subscription. If you choose a paid cloud model through OpenRouter, that provider may charge for its use.
